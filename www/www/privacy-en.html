GENIE

PRIVACY POLICY

Version 1.0 – Draft

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

The privacy of our users is very important to us.

Genie processes data only for purposes related to the use of the application, its features, security and communication between employees and employers.

Data processing is carried out in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable laws and regulations.

2. Who Is Responsible for Your Data?

Product: Genie

Data Controller: Bozidar Stankovic

Country: Sweden

Privacy Contact: An official email address will be published before launch.

3. What Data Do We Collect?

Depending on the features used by the user, Genie may process:

  • First and last name.
  • Email address and login information.
  • Employee and employer information.
  • Working-time and salary calculation data.
  • Hourly rates, supplements and pay periods.
  • Messages between employees and employers.
  • Information about connections and approvals between users.
  • Licence information and records of acceptance of the Terms of Use.
  • Technical data required for the operation and security of the application.

We do not collect data that is not necessary for the intended functionality of the application.

4. Why Do We Process Your Data?

Data is used for:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Calculating salaries and recording working time.
  • Connecting employees and employers.
  • Sending and receiving messages.
  • Storing users' salary calculations and records.
  • Managing licences and access to features.
  • Protecting the application against misuse.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the specific purpose, the legal basis for processing may be:

  • Performance of a contract with the user.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate interests in ensuring the security and proper functioning of the application.
  • The user's consent, where legally required.

An appropriate legal basis will be determined for each type of processing.

6. Data Storage and Security

Genie uses Firebase services for user authentication and storage of certain application data.

Technical and organisational security measures are applied, including access controls and database security rules.

Employee and employer data is not intended for public display. Access is restricted to authorised users in accordance with the application's features.

7. Sharing of Data

Data may be processed through technical service providers required for the operation of Genie, including Google Firebase.

Data may be displayed to a connected employee or employer only to the extent permitted by the application's features and the relevant access permissions.

We do not sell users' personal data to third parties.

8. International Data Transfers

Certain technical service providers may process data in different countries.

If data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, appropriate legal safeguards will be applied where required.

9. How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

Data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations and to handle legitimate claims.

Retention periods depend on the type and purpose of the data. Specific retention periods will be established before the application is launched.

10. User Rights

Under the GDPR, users may have the right to:

  • Receive information about how their data is processed.
  • Request access to their personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of data where the legal requirements are met.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Exercise the right to data portability where applicable.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be submitted using the official contact address.

11. Deletion of User Accounts

Users may request deletion of their accounts and personal data.

Deletion will be carried out in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, while respecting legal obligations to retain certain data.

12. Children

Genie is intended for business use by employees and employers and is not intended for children.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated due to changes in the application's features or legal requirements.

Users will be informed about significant changes.

14. Contact and Complaints

For questions and requests concerning the protection of personal data:

Data Controller: Bozidar Stankovic

Contact: An official email address will be published before launch.

Users who believe that their personal data is being processed in violation of applicable regulations may lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten – IMY), where it has jurisdiction.
